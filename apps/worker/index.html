Punch List by SMB Team

Mark it up. We’ll knock it out.

Click anywhere on your new site and tell us what needs fixing. Every note lands on your team’s list, and you watch each one get checked off.

How it works

SMB Team sends you a link to your new website. It opens in your browser with nothing to install and no account to create, on a computer or a phone.

  1. Pick the Comment tool in the toolbar, or press C.
  2. Click the spot on the page your note is about.
  3. Type what needs changing.
  4. Press Enter or click Post. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

Your note is pinned to the spot you clicked and lands on the team's punch list. Each one shows its status, from open to in progress to resolved, so you can watch your list get checked off. Reply on any note to keep the conversation in one place.

Review links are cleaned up 90 days after their last activity, and any comment or view resets that clock. Read thehelp page for the details, or contact your SMB Team project manager.
